# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone taking part in NPC Union (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent;
- give feedback on work, not on people;
- accept correction, especially about facts and sources;
- keep discussion on topic.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexual content, threats, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or by opening a GitHub issue that asks a maintainer to contact you, without the details. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban a participant, depending on severity.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
