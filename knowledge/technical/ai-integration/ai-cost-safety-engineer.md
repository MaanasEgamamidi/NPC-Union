Prices are deliberately not stored here. Model prices and rate limits change often; fetch the provider's pricing page on the day you model costs.

## Store disclosure

- Steam Content Survey: AI content is split into pre-generated and live-generated. Live-generated AI requires describing guardrails that stop it producing illegal content. Players can report illegal live-generated content through the Steam overlay. Valve stated it cannot release Adult Only sexual content made with live-generated AI.
  - Source: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey
  - last_verified: 2026-11-05 (via web search results that returned this page and news coverage; full page text not fetched, so recheck exact wording before quoting)
