# Unity Asset Store facts

last_verified: 2026-11-05

Facts below were checked by WebFetch on 2026-11-05 unless marked UNVERIFIED. Re-check anything older than 90 days before quoting it to a client.

## Sources

- Store front: https://assetstore.unity.com
- Store search: https://assetstore.unity.com/search
- Asset Store Terms of Service and EULA: https://unity.com/legal/as-terms

## Licence (from https://unity.com/legal/as-terms, checked 2026-11-05)

- Assets are licensed, not sold (EULA section 1.3).
- Standard EULA, non-Restricted Assets: non-exclusive, non-transferable, worldwide, perpetual licence to incorporate the asset into a Licensed Product (section 2.2.1).
- A Licensed Product must include substantial original content and have a purpose beyond displaying the assets (section 2.2.1(a)).
- Monetizing an asset within a Licensed Product, including through in-app purchases, is allowed (section 2.2.1(d)). Modification is allowed (section 2.2.1(e)).
- Not allowed without express authorization: monetizing an asset inside a product whose primary purpose is user-generated content (2.2.1.1(c)); letting your users commercialize the asset (2.2.1.1(b)); using assets in a digital representation of value or ownership, for example tokens (2.2.1.1(f)).
- No redistribution, resale or sublicensing of the asset itself (Terms 3.5, EULA 2.2.1.1(d)).
- No AI or ML training on assets without express consent (Terms 3.8(v), EULA 2.2.1.1(g)).
- No cost sharing so third parties can use one purchase (2.2.1.1(a)).
- SDKs may not be modified unless authorized (2.2.1.1(e)).

## Seats

- Extension Assets are assets in the "Editor Extension", "Scripting" or "Services" categories (section 2.3.2).
- Each Extension Asset licence is a single seat, usable on at most 2 computers, not shared or used concurrently on more than 2 computers (2.3.2).
- Build farms and VMs that only run, test or build projects do not need extra seats (2.3.2).
- Other (non-extension) assets may be used on unlimited computers owned by the licensee (2.3.1). A multi-entity tier extends use to Affiliates.
- Contractors need their own licences unless the asset is multi-entity (2.4).

## Restricted Assets

- Restricted Assets carry their own "Restricted Asset Terms" in the asset's materials, which override the EULA where they differ (2.2.2, 2.9). Always read them on the asset page. Route to asset-license-auditor.

## Store search (checked 2026-11-05)

- Top categories on the search page: 2D, 3D, Add-Ons, Audio, Templates, Tools, VFX.
- Store front menu also lists: SDKs, Verified Solutions, Sale.
- Search page facets seen: Price, Rating, Publisher, Release Date, plus sale facets "New release discount" and "On sale".
- [Unity's Asset Store guide](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.3/manual/assets-and-media/asset-store/downloads/asset-packages-purchase) lists sorting by relevance, popularity, rating, publish or update date, name, and price.
- Free assets: store front links "Top free assets" and "Top Free Assets". A dedicated free-only filter toggle: UNVERIFIED (Price facet may cover it).
- Unity version filter and render pipeline (Built-in, URP, HDRP) filter: UNVERIFIED as search facets on 2026-11-05. Read compatibility from each asset page instead.

## Asset page fields to read (UNVERIFIED layout, check per asset)

- Price, and sale price against list price when a sale is running.
- Licence type (Standard Unity Asset Store EULA, or Restricted Asset terms) and whether it is an Extension Asset.
- Supported Unity versions and render pipeline compatibility table.
- Latest version and release or update date.
- Publisher, rating count, file size.

## Pitfalls

- An asset tagged for Built-in may show pink materials in URP or HDRP. Check the pipeline table, not screenshots.
- "Last update" is not the same as "tested on your Unity version". Ask the publisher or test.
- Sale prices are time-limited. Record the date you saw the price.
