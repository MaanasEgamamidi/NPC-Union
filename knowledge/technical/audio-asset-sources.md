# Audio asset sources

Check current terms and individual file licences before use.

## Freesound

- URL: https://freesound.org
- FAQ: https://freesound.org/help/faq/ (checked 2026-11-05)
- Licence is set per sound: CC0, CC-BY (Attribution), or CC-BY-NC (Attribution Noncommercial).
- FAQ: CC0 allows nearly any use, including selling, but you cannot claim authorship.
- FAQ: CC-BY requires crediting the original creators.
- FAQ: CC-BY-NC means you cannot earn money from the work you make with the sound.
- Sampling+ is retired and not offered for new uploads; some older sounds still carry it. FAQ reads it as allowing most of what CC-BY-NC allows but not commercial advertising.
- FAQ gives a credit format: sound name, author, link to the sound page, licence.
- Check Freesound search help for current filters and exact field names before constructing a query.
- Read the licence and version on each sound page.

## Kevin MacLeod / incompetech

- URL: https://incompetech.com
- Licences page: https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/ (checked 2026-11-05)
- Free under Creative Commons with credit. The site generates the credit text.
- Paid Standard License for use without attribution. Price was not shown on the licences page.
- The [FAQ](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html) gives CC BY 4.0 attribution; check the specific track.

## OpenGameArt

- URL: https://opengameart.org
- Licence listed per submission. The [FAQ](https://opengameart.org/node/5571) discusses CC0, CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, GPL and OGA-BY.
- Share-alike and GPL may add duties to your release; send to asset-license-auditor.

## Sonniss GDC bundles

- URL: https://sonniss.com
- Check the [bundle licence](https://sonniss.com/gdc-bundle-license/) applicable on the download date and retain it with the download.

## itch.io audio packs

- URL: https://itch.io/game-assets/tag-sound-effects
- Licence set by each creator on the pack page. No licence text means do not use until the creator confirms in writing.

## YouTube Audio Library

- Check [YouTube Audio Library guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882) and the track licence for the intended game use; seek permission if it is not covered.

## Cross-source rules

- NC licences exclude commercial games, including free games with ads or IAP.
- CC-BY credit goes in the in-game credits or a shipped credits file.
- Content ID can flag streams even for licensed tracks. Keep proof of licence.
- Game music needs sync rights; selling a soundtrack also needs mechanical or distribution rights. A licence for videos may not cover a soundtrack release.
