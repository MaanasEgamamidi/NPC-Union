# Worldbuilding for games

## Method
1. Seed sentence with a pressure.
2. Geography → resources → trade → factions → history → rules.
3. Iceberg ledger: Shown / Discoverable / Hidden.

## Iceberg theory
Hemingway, Death in the Afternoon (1932), chapter 16: the dignity of an iceberg's movement is due to only one-eighth being above water; a writer who omits things because he does not know them only makes hollow places. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iceberg_theory

## Physical geography rules of thumb
- Rivers flow downhill to sea or lakes and generally merge rather than split going downstream (deltas excepted).
- Rain shadow: windward side of mountains is wet, leeward dry.
- Settlements cluster at fords, harbours, passes, confluences.

## Faction triangle
Controls / Lacks / Blocked by. Add "wants from player" for quest hooks.

## Game examples (checked)
