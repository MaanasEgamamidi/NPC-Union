# Design reference and NPC research sources

- [Getty Open Content Program](https://www.getty.edu/projects/open-content-program/), last_verified: 2026-11-05: Getty describes its Open Content images of public-domain works as available without restrictions or fees. Check the individual item's status and any other applicable rights before reuse. A link used as inspiration is not proof that a new asset is cleared.
- [Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior](https://hci.stanford.edu/publications/paper.php?id=482), last_verified: 2026-11-05: the authors' research describes a simulated setting with agents that retrieve memories and plan. It is a research prototype, not evidence that a production game will meet a particular cost, latency, or reliability target.
- [OpenAI: Understanding prompt injections](https://openai.com/safety/prompt-injections/), last_verified: 2026-11-05: external content can contain instructions intended to redirect an agent; limit access and use layered safeguards. Adapt this general principle to player input and NPC memories. No prompt guarantees safety.

The design rules in the agents and skills are proposed heuristics, not measurements or claims from these sources.
