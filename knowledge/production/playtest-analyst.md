# Playtest analyst reference notes

- Nielsen recommends iterative usability tests with five users based on a model of diminishing returns; this is a heuristic, not a guarantee for every study. [Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/why-you-only-need-to-test-with-5-users/).
- SUS has 10 items with five response options and a score from 0 to 100; see [Lewis and Sauro](https://measuringu.com/papers/Lewis_Sauro_HCII2009.pdf) for wording and scoring.
- GEQ and PENS licensing or permission for commercial use may differ; check with the authors or rights holders. UNVERIFIED.
- Rules on recording minors and retaining video or biometric data vary by country and platform; route to game-legal-advisor. UNVERIFIED.
- Remote unmoderated testing platforms and their pricing change often; check vendor pages directly. UNVERIFIED.
