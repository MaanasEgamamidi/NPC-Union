# QA lead reference notes

Version and policy-sensitive facts. Each item is UNVERIFIED until checked against the source on the date of use.

- Sony TRC, Xbox Requirements (XR) and Nintendo submission guidelines are distributed under NDA through each platform's developer portal. Check the current revision before every submission. UNVERIFIED.
- Patch and title update resubmission rules differ by platform and change over time. Confirm in the portal. UNVERIFIED.
- Platform cert turnaround times are not public and vary by season and queue. Plan for at least one failure and resubmission. UNVERIFIED.
- Steam Deck Verified reviews games on four categories: input, display, "seamlessness" and system support. Results are Verified, Playable, Unsupported or Unknown. UNVERIFIED (check the Steamworks Steam Deck compatibility page).
- IARC issues ratings for several digital storefronts through a single questionnaire. Which stores participate changes. UNVERIFIED.
- ISTQB glossary terms (entry criteria, exit criteria, regression testing, smoke testing) are the shared vocabulary used in plans. UNVERIFIED for current syllabus version.
