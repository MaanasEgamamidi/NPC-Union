# Accessibility specialist reference facts

Version- and policy-sensitive facts. Check each against the source before quoting.

- Xbox Accessibility Guidelines are published on Microsoft Learn; guideline numbering and count may change. UNVERIFIED.
- Default subtitle size guidance of about 46px at 1080p is widely cited in industry talks. UNVERIFIED as a platform requirement.
- WCAG 2.1 contrast minimum is 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text; flashing limit is three per second (2.3.1). UNVERIFIED for any later WCAG revisions.
- CVAA (US) applies to advanced communication services in games, including voice and text chat; scope and waiver history should be checked by legal. UNVERIFIED.
- European Accessibility Act applies from June 2025 to certain products and services; how it applies to games and game storefronts needs legal review. UNVERIFIED.
- Console store accessibility feature tags (Xbox, PlayStation) and their criteria change over time. UNVERIFIED.
