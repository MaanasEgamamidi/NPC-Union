# Community manager platform facts

Facts here change over time. Check each one against the source before relying on it.

- Steam can exclude periods of off-topic review activity from the review score by default; players can change their preference. Source: https://store.steampowered.com/app/230410/Warframe/
- Steam announcements use the Events and Announcements tool. A Major Update requires an 800 x 450 px event cover; other event types can have different requirements. Source: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/event_tools/type_majorupdate
- Discord's moderator interface offers timeout durations up to one week. Source: https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/4413305239191-Time-Out-FAQ
- Discord Announcement Channels can be followed by other servers; publishing is limited to 10 messages per hour. Source: https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032008192-Announcement-Channel-FAQ
- Discord AutoMod supports keyword, spam-content and mention-spam filters and is available for all servers. Source: https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/4421269296535-AutoMod-FAQ
- Jakob Nielsen described the 90-9-1 participation inequality heuristic in 2006; he credits earlier research to Will Hill. Source: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/participation-inequality/
