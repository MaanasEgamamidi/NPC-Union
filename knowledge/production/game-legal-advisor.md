Legal information, not legal advice. Unchecked facts below are marked UNVERIFIED; check current primary sources before relying on them.

## Engines

- Unity cancelled the Runtime Fee in September 2024. Unity says the Personal financial threshold increased to $200,000 with Unity 6; check current eligibility terms. [Unity pricing updates](https://unity.com/products/pricing-updates). Current splash-screen rules: UNVERIFIED.
- Unreal Engine generally charges a 5% royalty on royalty revenue, with a $1 million lifetime gross revenue exclusion per product and other exclusions; eligible Launch Everywhere with Epic releases can receive a reduced rate. See the [current EULA](https://www.unrealengine.com/eula/unreal) for definitions, eligibility and reporting.
- Godot is MIT licensed; ship its licence text and third-party notices. UNVERIFIED for the current list of bundled third-party components, check godotengine.org/license.

## Ratings

- IARC lists participating digital storefronts and the regional ratings displayed on its [storefronts page](https://globalratings.com/storefronts/).
- ESRB introduced its "In-Game Purchases (Includes Random Items)" notice in February 2020 ([ESRB](https://www.esrb.org/blog/what-parents-need-to-know-about-loot-boxes-and-other-in-game-purchases/)). PEGI current wording: UNVERIFIED.
- Apple uses its own age rating system: global ratings include 4+, 9+, 13+, 16+ and 18+, with regional and older-OS variations. [Apple age ratings](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions).

## Loot boxes

- Belgium: Gaming Commission treated paid loot boxes as gambling (2018). Current enforcement: UNVERIFIED.
- Netherlands: Council of State overturned the EA FIFA fine (2022). Current position: UNVERIFIED.
- Australia: chance-based in-game purchases, including paid loot boxes, lead to at least M, and simulated gambling to R 18+, from 22 September 2024. [Australian Classification](https://www.classification.gov.au/about-us/media-and-news/news/new-classifications-for-gambling-content-video-games).
- [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) and [Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/18265346?hl=en-ph) require odds disclosure before purchases of randomized virtual items. China requirements: UNVERIFIED.

- GDPR Article 8: default age 16, member states may set 13 to 15. Per-country ages: UNVERIFIED.
