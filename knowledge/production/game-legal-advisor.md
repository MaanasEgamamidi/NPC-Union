Legal information, not legal advice. Check current primary sources before relying on them.

## Engines

- Unity cancelled the Runtime Fee in September 2024. Unity says the Personal financial threshold increased to $200,000 with Unity 6; check current eligibility terms. [Unity pricing updates](https://unity.com/products/pricing-updates). Check current splash-screen rules in the relevant Unity version's documentation.
- Unreal Engine generally charges a 5% royalty on royalty revenue, with a $1 million lifetime gross revenue exclusion per product and other exclusions; eligible Launch Everywhere with Epic releases can receive a reduced rate. See the [current EULA](https://www.unrealengine.com/eula/unreal) for definitions, eligibility and reporting.
- Godot is MIT licensed; ship its licence text and third-party notices. Check the [current bundled third-party component notices](https://godotengine.org/license/) for the version shipped.

## Ratings

- IARC lists participating digital storefronts and the regional ratings displayed on its [storefronts page](https://globalratings.com/storefronts/).
- ESRB introduced its "In-Game Purchases (Includes Random Items)" notice in February 2020 ([ESRB](https://www.esrb.org/blog/what-parents-need-to-know-about-loot-boxes-and-other-in-game-purchases/)). Check current PEGI wording on the [PEGI site](https://pegi.info/).
- Apple uses its own age rating system: global ratings include 4+, 9+, 13+, 16+ and 18+, with regional and older-OS variations. [Apple age ratings](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions).

## Loot boxes

- For Belgium and the Netherlands, check current regulator guidance and court decisions with local counsel before shipping paid random items.
- Australia: chance-based in-game purchases, including paid loot boxes, lead to at least M, and simulated gambling to R 18+, from 22 September 2024. [Australian Classification](https://www.classification.gov.au/about-us/media-and-news/news/new-classifications-for-gambling-content-video-games).
- [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) and [Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/18265346?hl=en-ph) require odds disclosure before purchases of randomized virtual items. Check current Chinese requirements with local counsel.

## Child privacy

- COPPA (US, under 13). Epic's December 2022 FTC settlement: 275 million US dollars COPPA penalty plus 245 million refunds. Check the [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/) for current COPPA rule dates and compliance deadlines.
- GDPR Article 8: default age 16, member states may set 13 to 15. Check each target country's implementing law for its age threshold.
- UK Age Appropriate Design Code covers under-18s. Check the [ICO](https://ico.org.uk/) for current guidance updates.
