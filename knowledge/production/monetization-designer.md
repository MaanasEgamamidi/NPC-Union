# Monetization policy and pricing facts

Facts that change over time. Check each against the primary source before relying on it.

- Apple App Review Guidelines 3.1.1 require apps offering purchased randomized virtual items to disclose the odds of each item type before purchase. [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
- Google Play: randomized paid items require odds disclosure before purchase. UNVERIFIED (check Payments policy).
- Belgium: Gaming Commission treated paid loot boxes as gambling in 2018. UNVERIFIED (check current enforcement).
- Netherlands: Council of State overturned the regulator's loot box fine against EA in 2022. UNVERIFIED (check for newer legislation).
- Australia: from 22 September 2024, games with chance-based in-game purchases, including paid loot boxes, receive at least M; simulated gambling receives R 18+. [Australian Classification](https://www.classification.gov.au/about-us/media-and-news/news/new-classifications-for-gambling-content-video-games).
- China: probability disclosure for random paid items required since 2017. UNVERIFIED (check current rules).
- EU CPC Network key principles on in-game virtual currencies, 2024: show real-money prices, avoid currency obfuscation. UNVERIFIED.
- EU Omnibus Directive: price reductions must reference the lowest price in the prior 30 days. UNVERIFIED for digital game storefronts.
- FTC and Epic Games settlements announced in December 2022 totaled $520 million; $245 million concerned billing and alleged dark patterns. [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/508FY2023FTCAgencyFinancialReport11292023.pdf).
- Steam generally offers refunds for games requested within 14 days of purchase and under two hours of play; separate conditions apply to DLC and in-game purchases. [Steam refunds](https://store.steampowered.com/steam_refunds/).
- Steam moved Argentina and Turkey to USD regional pricing in 2023. UNVERIFIED.
- Steam discount rules and cooldowns between discounts. UNVERIFIED (check Steamworks discounting docs).
- PEGI and ESRB descriptor "In-Game Purchases (Includes Random Items)". UNVERIFIED (check current label wording).
