# Producer reference facts

Policy and version sensitive facts. Check each against the live source before quoting it.

- Steam requires a Coming Soon page to be visible for at least two weeks before release. [Release options](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/types).
- Steam Next Fest runs three times a year and requires a publicly playable demo by the start. Check the edition-specific deadlines and dates in [Steamworks](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest).
- Console certification requirements (Sony TRC, Xbox requirements, Nintendo guidelines) live in NDA partner portals. Turnaround times UNVERIFIED; plan one submission plus one resubmission.
- Age rating (ESRB, PEGI, IARC) timing affects storefront listing. Process details UNVERIFIED; confirm with game-legal-advisor.
- Hades (Supergiant Games): early access December 2018, 1.0 in September 2020. Verify exact dates on the Steam or Supergiant pages before publishing.
